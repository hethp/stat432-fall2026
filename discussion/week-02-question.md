**Question:**

For a nested sequence of least-squares models with $p = 1, ..., P predictors, explain why the number of predictors that minimizes MSE(f(x_0)) at a single fixed point x0 can differ from the number that minimizes average test MSE over the design. Focus specifically on how the variance term at x0 depends on x0's leverage, and give one concrete example where this causes the two criteria to disagree.
